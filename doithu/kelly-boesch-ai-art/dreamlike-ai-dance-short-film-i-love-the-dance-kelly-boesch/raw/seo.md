--- DESCRIPTION ---
I wanted to combine fashion and dance for this one. Midjourney sometimes makes the same person repeated in scenes. Also, when I use my personalization code, which I do almost always, I get women with no hair or pulled back hair. I could ask for long hair but I kind of like the focus being on the fashion. Some of the women almost resemble mannequins. I didn't ask for that in the prompt. I find it interesting how Midjourney interprets the idea and sometimes just go with it. Animated using VEO3. Song made using @suno , my lyrics. The name of the song is "I Love The Dance".
