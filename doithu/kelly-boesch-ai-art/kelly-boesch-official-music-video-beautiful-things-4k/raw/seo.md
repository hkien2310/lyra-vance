--- DESCRIPTION ---
I wrote these lyrics about how I always get lost in time enjoying beauty. Watching the hummingbirds, waiting for the sunset, spending hours looking for four leaf clovers....of which I have found many over the years...including a few five leaf clovers. The hair on the characters is sort of a joke to myself and my crazy hair. Many years ago I dyed my hair flame red. Not as red as these characters but pretty red and it was big and curly. My hair can get pretty wild. In my mind I feel like it looks like some of these characters hair, although now I'm more blondish. The song is called 'Beautiful Things' and I hope it puts you in as good of a mood as it does me. Makes me happy. :-)
