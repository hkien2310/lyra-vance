Sitting at the carved walnut tea table, one old man lifts the porcelain teapot to pour warm tea for his friend as their white beard and halo of silver hair sway gently in the breeze.
A sweeping coastal breeze moves through the green sea-grass, golden buttercups, and white daisies while warm light glows from the lantern tower above the open parchment-page walls of the storybook lighthouse. Sitting close together on the teak loveseat, the elderly couple lean their silver heads tenderly together as the husband rests his hand over his wife's and the two fluffy white sheep shift calmly in the grass beside them.
