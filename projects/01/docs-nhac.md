### 1. Song Title
`The Rhythm Stayed Home`

### 2. Suno Style Prompt (80 words)
```text
112 BPM, C minor lifting to Eb major, uplifting stomping gospel-soul and tribal body-percussion dance anthem. Quiet close-mic spoken word intro building into barefoot floor stomps, crisp rhythmic handclaps, and warm hand drums. Dramatic stop-time pre-chorus breaks on the word "Wait!". Soulful raspy mature female lead vocal trading energetic call-and-response chants ("Hey!", "Who kept it? We did!") with a joyful choir, emotional stripped-back spoken bridge over warm strings, explosive celebratory final chorus, wide dynamic analog mix.
```
